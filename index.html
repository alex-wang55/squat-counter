How scoring works
  • Perfect squat (deep) = 25 pts · Great = 15 pts · Good = 10 pts · shallow = 5 pts
  • Chain good-or-better reps for a streak bonus every 3 in a row.
  • Your best score is saved on this device, so you can beat it next time.
Tips for best tracking
  • Stand far enough back that your hips, knees, and ankles are all visible.
  • A side-on (profile) view tracks squat depth most accurately.
  • Make sure you're well-lit — backlighting confuses the model.
  • If your legs go out of frame (e.g. sitting down), tracking pauses on purpose — reps won't be counted until your full leg is back in view.
